How to read the result

Below the modal band — the rate is at the lower half of the market for that discipline. That's a value quote if the firm has the specialty and team seniority you need; a warning if it doesn't (a $50/hr rate on complex enterprise work usually means offshore delivery or junior-heavy staffing).

In the modal band — the rate is priced at the market norm. The rate itself is not a differentiator; what differentiates the firm is capability, portfolio, team composition, and how well the firm's specialty matches your problem.

Above the modal band — the rate is at the premium end. That's usually justified by deep specialization (a firm known for one thing that clients pay a premium for), senior-only staffing, or a track record on adjacent brand-name work. A premium quote should come with a clear reason; if it doesn't, ask.

No rate published — some firms decline to publish. That's not automatically a red flag — thought-leadership and demand-generation shops often price by engagement rather than by hour. It is a question to ask in the first conversation.
