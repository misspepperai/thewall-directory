What Pipedrive is

Pipedrive is a sales-focused CRM headquartered in Tallinn, Estonia, founded in 2010. Its differentiator is the drag-and-drop deal pipeline UI — sales-first rather than the marketing-first model of HubSpot or the enterprise complexity of Salesforce. Owned by Vista Equity Partners since 2020.

Who uses Pipedrive

Small and mid-market sales teams (typically 3–50 reps) who want a CRM that reps actually update — because updating it is one click, not a form. Not the right choice for marketing-led motions (HubSpot wins) or enterprise complexity (Salesforce wins).

What Pipedrive costs

Essential ~$15/user/month, Advanced ~$29, Professional ~$59, Enterprise ~$99. Most SMB sales orgs run Advanced or Professional. Add-ons for lead nurturing (LeadBooster), Web Visitors, Campaigns, Projects price separately.

Where Pipedrive sits in the growth-vendor context

Pipedrive shows up on shortlists in The Wall's Sales pillar. Buyers who consider Pipedrive typically also evaluate HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho CRM — the shortlist question is usually not whether Pipedrive is a good product (it generally is, or it wouldn't be on the list), but whether its center of gravity fits the company's shape.

Frequently asked

What is Pipedrive?

Pipedrive is a CRM built visually around the sales pipeline — deals move through stages via drag-and-drop, and every feature is designed to reduce friction on reps updating their deals.

How much does Pipedrive cost?

From ~$15/user/month (Essential) to ~$99/user/month (Enterprise). Most mid-market sales teams run Advanced (~$29) or Professional (~$59).

Pipedrive vs HubSpot vs Salesforce?

Pipedrive is sales-first and cheapest. HubSpot is marketing-and-sales-unified and mid-market-optimized. Salesforce is enterprise-first and most customizable. Match the tool to your primary motion.

Is Pipedrive good for a small business?

Yes — Pipedrive is one of the two or three best-fit CRMs for SMB sales teams under 20 reps. Fast to deploy, cheap per seat, and reps actually use it.

What are Pipedrive alternatives?

HubSpot Sales Hub (unified with marketing), Close (calling-focused), Copper (Google Workspace-native), Zoho CRM (cheaper), Salesforce (enterprise).
