What Customer.io is

Customer.io is a Portland-based, fully-remote SaaS company founded in 2012. The product specializes in behavior-triggered messaging across email, SMS, push, and in-app — driven by event data (what users did) rather than static list membership. Popular in SaaS, e-commerce, and any product where user behavior varies enough to justify per-user messaging.

Who uses Customer.io

Product-led SaaS companies, mobile app operators, and e-commerce brands that need to trigger messages from product events. Requires event tracking (Segment, custom integration) to be useful — not a plug-and-play SMB tool like Mailchimp.

What Customer.io costs

Essentials ~$100/month for up to 5K profiles + email. Premium (custom pricing) adds SMS, push, in-app, and advanced features. Most real deployments end up in the $200–$2,000/month range depending on profile volume and channel mix.

Frequently asked

What is Customer.io?

Customer.io is a customer messaging platform for behavior-triggered email, SMS, push, and in-app messages — driven by event data about what users actually do in your product.

How much does Customer.io cost?

Essentials starts at ~$100/month (5K profiles, email). Premium (custom) adds SMS/push/in-app. Real deployments typically $200–$2,000/month.

Customer.io vs Braze vs Iterable?

All three are behavior-triggered messaging platforms. Braze and Iterable are enterprise-first with heavier pricing and deeper mobile capabilities. Customer.io is mid-market-first, developer-friendly, and roughly a third the cost for equivalent volumes.

Do I need a CDP with Customer.io?

A CDP or clean event tracking makes Customer.io dramatically more valuable — the platform is only as good as the event data flowing in. Most Customer.io deployments run alongside Segment or RudderStack.

What are Customer.io alternatives?

Braze (enterprise), Iterable (enterprise), Klaviyo (e-commerce specific), OneSignal (mobile-first), Intercom (support-first), HubSpot Marketing Hub (if you're already on HubSpot).
