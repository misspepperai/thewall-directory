Platform reference

The platforms that show up repeatedly in US growth-vendor conversations. 29 reference pages so far, grouped by the pillar each platform anchors. Facts, pricing bands, alternatives, and how the platform fits into a real vendor shortlist — same standards as the rest of the atlas.

Automation

HubSpot

The dominant all-in-one CRM + marketing + sales platform for the mid-market.

Marketo Engage

Adobe's enterprise B2B marketing automation platform, historically the standard for lead management at scale.

ActiveCampaign

The strongest all-in-one marketing automation platform for small and mid-market businesses.

Klaviyo

The dominant marketing automation platform for e-commerce, deeply integrated with Shopify.

Mailchimp

The oldest and most widely-deployed email marketing platform, now owned by Intuit.

Segment

The most widely-adopted customer data platform (CDP), owned by Twilio.

Google Analytics 4

The default web and app analytics platform for the internet, replacing Universal Analytics as of 2023.

Zapier

The dominant no-code workflow automation platform, connecting 6,000+ apps.

Make

The visual, branching-first automation platform — the technical alternative to Zapier.

Customer.io

The developer-friendly customer messaging platform for behavior-triggered email, SMS, and push.

Sales

Salesforce

The dominant enterprise CRM platform and the anchor of most large B2B revenue stacks.

Outreach.io

The dominant sales-engagement platform for B2B sales development and outbound teams.

Pipedrive

The visual, pipeline-first CRM built for small and mid-market sales teams.

Zoho CRM

The comprehensive CRM at a fraction of Salesforce or HubSpot pricing, part of the broader Zoho One suite.

ZoomInfo

The enterprise-scale B2B contact and company database, with intent data and workflow tools.

Apollo.io

The all-in-one sales intelligence + engagement platform that made ZoomInfo-level data affordable to SMBs.

Demand Gen

Google Ads

The world's dominant paid-search platform and the anchor of most US B2B and B2C paid-media stacks.

Meta Ads Manager

The primary interface for paid advertising on Facebook and Instagram — the second-largest ad platform in the world.

LinkedIn Campaign Manager

The primary paid-advertising platform for reaching B2B buying committees at work.

The Trade Desk

The dominant independent demand-side platform (DSP) for programmatic advertising across the open web.

Instantly.ai

The cold-email automation platform built for high-volume outbound at low cost.

SEO

Ahrefs

The most-used all-in-one SEO toolset for keyword research, backlink analysis, and content strategy.

Semrush

The other dominant all-in-one SEO and digital-marketing toolset, spanning SEO, PPC, content, and social.

Content Marketing

Beehiiv

The newsletter platform built for creators who want to grow, monetize, and treat their newsletter like a business.

Substack

The reader-network-first paid newsletter and podcast platform that defined the modern independent-publisher renaissance.

AI Marketing

ChatGPT (OpenAI)

The dominant general-purpose AI assistant and the catalyst for the generative-AI adoption wave in marketing.

Claude (Anthropic)

The AI assistant most-preferred for writing quality, long-context work, and coding by serious knowledge-work users.

Perplexity

The AI-native search engine that cites sources, competing directly with Google as a knowledge-work default.

Jasper

The enterprise-focused AI content platform built for marketing teams with brand guidelines and workflow needs.
