When to pick each

PICK BEEHIIVYou are running a newsletter as a business — you want to keep 100% of revenue, add ads, own your monetization, and build with team members. The 10% Substack takes is real money once you cross $10K MRR.

PICK SUBSTACKYou are just starting, need discovery help, and want the simplest possible setup. The Substack Network genuinely gets new writers subscribers faster than any alternative — worth the 10% until you can grow without it.

Why this comparison matters

Both Beehiiv and Substack show up on nearly every content marketing shortlist in 2026, which is why the "vs" query gets asked constantly and answered badly.
