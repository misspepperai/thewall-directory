When to pick each

PICK NOTIONYou need a wiki, team knowledge base, docs, or meeting-notes hub. Notion's document-first design makes long-form collaboration natural in a way Airtable can't match.

PICK AIRTABLEYou need structured operational data — a content calendar, campaign tracker, editorial pipeline, or lightweight CRM. Airtable's database power and interface builder actually deliver working ops systems.

Why this comparison matters

Both Notion and Airtable show up on nearly every marketing automation shortlist in 2026, which is why the "vs" query gets asked constantly and answered badly.
