Is content marketing worth it?

For businesses selling considered products or services where buyers research before purchasing — B2B software, professional services, high-ticket B2C — content marketing is usually the most durable growth channel available. It compounds; paid ads deliver only when you're paying. The catch is that it takes 6–18 months to see meaningful traffic and requires editorial consistency most orgs struggle to maintain.
