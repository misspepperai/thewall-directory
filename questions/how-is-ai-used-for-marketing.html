How is AI used for marketing?

AI has replaced or accelerated four categories of marketing work: content production (drafting, editing, repurposing), personalization (segmenting audiences, dynamic email content, ad variants), analysis (extracting insights from CRM and web data), and creative production (image, video, voice). The teams getting real leverage aren't "using AI" — they're rebuilding their content and campaign workflows around AI as the default first draft.
