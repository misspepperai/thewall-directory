What is sales automation?

Sales automation is software that removes manual, repetitive work from the sales process — email sequencing, meeting scheduling, follow-up reminders, data entry into CRM, deal-stage progression, quote generation. Modern sales automation platforms (Outreach, Salesloft, Apollo, HubSpot Sales Hub) combine sequencing, dialing, and analytics into one workflow. The point isn't replacing salespeople — it's freeing them from tasks that don't require judgment so they spend more time on tasks that do.
