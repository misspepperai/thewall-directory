Is a marketing agency worth it?

A marketing agency is worth it when your monthly marketing capacity gap costs more than the retainer. Concretely: if you need 2 senior specialists (SEO + paid, say) to run a channel properly, in-house that's $180K+/year all-in. A specialist agency at $8K/mo is $96K/year with more collective experience across their client base. The math flips against agencies at enterprise scale where in-house teams gain economies you can't rent.
