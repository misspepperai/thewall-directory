Why the offer exists

Miss Pepper AI builds and runs AI-native marketing systems — SEO, content, automation, and analytics — for businesses whose complexity has outgrown execution-by-agency. That description does not fit every client who lands in a growth-services firm's inbox. Frequently, the fit sits somewhere adjacent: the client wants an AI system, not a service. Or an integrated stack, not a channel specialist. Or a partner who owns the whole marketing function.

When that mismatch happens — every established agency has this happen dozens of times a quarter — most agencies decline the work politely and lose the conversation. We'd like to convert those conversations into revenue for both of us instead. That's the entire program.

How it works

  1. Sign up. Email support@misspepper.ai saying you're in. We add you to the referral partner list. No forms, no NDA, no minimum commitment.
  2. You keep doing your work. When a lead lands that isn't your fit — wrong scope, wrong shape, wrong service model — you introduce them to us with a two-line email. That's your entire effort.
  3. We onboard the client. If they sign, we notify you the same day with the retainer amount and start date.
  4. We pay you 20% of every retainer payment we receive from that client. Monthly, via ACH or Stripe. For as long as they remain a Miss Pepper client. First check within 30 days of their first payment to us.

The terms in plain language

Rate
20% of the actual monthly retainer we receive from the referred client, before any fees. Paid monthly.
Duration
For the life of the client relationship with Miss Pepper AI. Not a first-year cap, not a 12-month cap — the whole account.
What counts as a referral
An email introducing us to a specific named contact at a real prospective client, sent by you (as the partner) to support@misspepper.ai. Cold leads passed in bulk with no personal intro don't qualify — this is a warm-intro program.
Attribution window
If a referred lead signs within 12 months of the intro, the referral counts. After 12 months of no contract, the referral expires.
Cost to you
Zero. No signup fee, no monthly fee, no minimum volume. No penalty for referring nothing in a given quarter.
Exclusivity
None. You can refer to as many partners as you want; we don't restrict who you work with. We just ask that when you refer to us, you refer to us — not to a competitor at the same time.
Client protection
We don't compete with you on the work you already do. If the referred client asks us to take over services you provide, we tell you first and either decline or split the engagement — your choice.
Termination
Either side can end the partnership at any time by email. Referrals already made continue to pay out.
Payment mechanics
ACH (US) or Stripe Connect. First check within 30 days of the client's first payment to us. Monthly thereafter. W-9 required for US partners.

Frequently asked

Why is Miss Pepper AI doing this through The Wall?

Because The Wall's directory is where the vendors we want to partner with already are. We built it as an industry reference; now we're using it as the introduction to a partnership offer. Full disclosure of the ownership relationship lives on our disclosures page.

What kind of clients do you actually serve?

Established US businesses past $5M in revenue with 25+ employees, in service industries where AI-native marketing systems produce compounding leverage — home services, IT/MSP, attorneys, B2B services, healthcare-adjacent. We're a systems company, not a channel specialist. If a lead wants "please run our Facebook ads" we're the wrong fit; if a lead wants "please rebuild how our whole marketing function actually operates," that's us.

Do you white-label services for other agencies?

Not currently. This program is direct: you introduce us to the client, the client signs with Miss Pepper AI directly, we pay you 20% of what they pay us. Cleaner and more durable than white-labeling.

What if my client I refer already has heard of Miss Pepper AI?

Prior awareness doesn't disqualify a referral. What qualifies it is that you made the specific warm intro that led to the current conversation. If they were already in a live sales conversation with us before your intro, we'll tell you and neither party pays.

Do I need to update my listing in The Wall?

No — the partnership is separate from your listing. Your listing stays exactly as it is (or you can update it any time through our contact page). Being listed and being a partner are independent facts.

Can I refer clients even if I'm not listed in The Wall?

Yes — this program is open to anyone in the growth-services industry, listed or not. Being in the directory is how we're introducing the program; it's not a prerequisite. Same terms either way.

Ready to be added to the referral partner list?

Email support@misspepper.ai with your company name, your name, and the best email for us to reach you when we have a referral to route. We add partners the same business day.
