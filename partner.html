Frequently asked

How is Miss Pepper AI connected to The Wall?

We operate it. Fully disclosed on the disclosures page. The directory's editorial standards — no paid inclusion, no ranked placement, no editorial preference for partners — are unaffected by this deal.

Am I locked in?

No. Either side can end the affiliate at any time by email. Payments on referrals already made continue for the life of the account.

What if we compete on something?

If we compete, don't refer that piece of work — refer the pieces you don't do. Most vendors in The Wall aren't in the AI-marketing-systems business we're in, so this rarely comes up. When it does, you decide what to send.

Does being a partner change anything about my listing?

No. Your listing stays alphabetical in its category, un-ranked, no "featured partner" badge. Partner status confers zero directory advantage — that's the whole point of keeping the directory independent.

What kind of clients do you actually work with?

US businesses past $5M in revenue, 25+ employees, service industries (home services, IT/MSP, professional services, B2B). We build the AI marketing stack and run the systems. Typical retainer is $8K–$18K/month. If a client wants channel-specific execution, they're a fit for you, not us.

Can partners without a Wall listing join?

Yes — this deal is open to anyone in the growth-services space, whether you're in the directory or not. Same 10/20 either way.
