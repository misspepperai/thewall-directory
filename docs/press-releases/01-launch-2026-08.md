## The Wall opens with 2,286 verified US growth-services vendors and publishes its data standards on day one

### An independent directory built for executives past the "which agency should we hire?" wall — no rankings, no pay-to-play, no fabricated statistics

**TAMPA, FL — August 4, 2026** — [The Wall](https://hitthewall.net) launched publicly today with 2,286 verified US-based growth-services vendors across 10 category pillars: marketing, SEO, creative, sales, content, social media, automation, demand-generation, AI marketing, and thought-leadership firms. Every listed company is US-headquartered, machine-verified as live before publication, and structured as a data record rather than marketing copy — hourly-rate band, minimum project size, team size, founding year, headquarters, and client-review data are published where the vendor has disclosed them.

Published by [Miss Pepper AI](https://misspepper.ai), The Wall is aimed at executives and operators at US companies past roughly $5M in revenue who have hit a wall in sales, marketing, thought leadership, or creative — and want the shortest path to a shortlist without wading through pay-to-play listicles.

The launch index carries 1,738 listings with provider-declared engagement data. Of the 1,440 firms disclosing an hourly rate, seven in ten (69.5%) price between $100 and $199 per hour — a tighter market center than most buyers expect. Only 6.6% of disclosing firms price below $50 an hour, and only 1.5% price above $300. The full rate distribution is published at [hitthewall.net/data](https://hitthewall.net/data), a browser-based interactive explorer that slices every aggregate by pillar, state, rate band, team size, and founding decade.

### Data standards, not "best of" lists

The Wall does not accept payment for placement, ranking, or removal. Listings are alphabetical within categories. During pre-launch verification, 696 companies were removed from the raw build — 668 headquartered outside the United States and 28 whose websites failed repeated liveness checks. Removed domains are barred from automated re-entry. The full editorial standards, corrections policy, and independence disclosure are published at [hitthewall.net/editorial-policy.html](https://hitthewall.net/editorial-policy.html).

The site publishes 22 original data briefings computed entirely from the live directory. Recent titles include the state of pricing in the US growth-services market, the concentration of vendors by US state (California leads at 313, followed by New York, Texas, and Florida), and the 182 firms in the index founded before 2000 — including six founded before 1970.

"Most marketing directories exist to help sellers, not buyers.
