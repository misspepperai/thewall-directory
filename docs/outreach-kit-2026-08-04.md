# The Wall — Briefings Outreach Kit
*Version 1 · 2026-08-04*

## What this is

The Wall (hitthewall.net) has published 18 original data briefings on the US growth-vendor market as of August 2026, all computed from the directory's own 2,286-listing dataset — no fabricated numbers, no vendor-sponsored takes, methodology disclosed on every article. This kit exists so someone with an inbox and 60 minutes a week can turn those briefings into press mentions, backlinks, and roundup inclusions.

Use it two ways:
1. **Reactive** — as a HARO / Connectively / Muckrack response bank. Journalist posts a query on B2B marketing, agency pricing, marketing automation, etc.; grab the matching angle and paste-adapt the response template.
2. **Proactive** — as a direct-pitch playbook to a target list of trade-press reporters.

Everything below is copy-adapt-ready. Every claim in every angle sheet is linked to a specific published briefing so a reporter can verify.

---

## Section 1 — The house style for every pitch

**Byline attribution:** "The Wall Editorial Team" (Organization), no individual persona. Do not attach a fake author name. If a reporter needs a quotable human, offer Dan Kurtz, Founder/Editor of The Wall (support@misspepper.ai).

**The Wall's positioning in one sentence:** *"The Wall is an independent, US-only directory of 2,286 verified growth-services vendors — the operations atlas for companies past $5M in revenue whose leadership has hit a wall in sales, marketing, thought leadership, or creative."*

**Data source disclosure:** every figure comes from either the directory's own listings (Clutch-sourced provider-declared engagement data on 1,738 firms), or from cited external sources (DataForSEO for search-trend claims). Never present modeled numbers as measured.

**What NOT to promise:** access to individual listed vendors, custom-cuts of data on request without vetting, blog placements, guest posts on The Wall in exchange for coverage, or paid placement in the directory (inclusion cannot be bought — this is a load-bearing standard, don't compromise it).

## Section 6 — Weekly outreach cadence (recommended)

**Monday (30 min):** Scan Connectively / Muckrack / SourceBottle for the week's queries. Match 2–3 against Section 3 response bank. Send.

**Wednesday (30 min):** One Template A cold pitch to a Tier 1 reporter, keyed to whichever briefing is most recent. Rotate outlets weekly.

**Friday (30 min):** One Template B follow-up to a reporter who's recently covered a topic our briefings support. Search "[topic] site:[outlet].com" to find fresh coverage.

Total: ~1.5 hours per week. Expected pace once dialed in: 1–2 mentions per month by month 3.
