What it does for you


The badge links to your listing at hitthewall.net/c/{your-domain}.html — a page with your company profile, the discipline you're in, your published rate and team-size data (if you've disclosed it on Clutch), and structured JSON-LD that helps search engines and AI answer surfaces cite you. Your listing already ranks; the badge on your own site reinforces the reciprocal connection.



Why we made this


Two reasons. First, listed vendors deserve a way to publicize the fact they're indexed — same reason "Featured in Forbes" badges exist. Second, a link back from your site to your Wall listing helps your listing's search visibility (and reciprocally, our directory's), which benefits everyone.



The badge, the link, and the listing itself are all free. No paid tiers, no premium badge, no upgrade. Corrections to your listing: reply to the email that reached you or use the contact page. Read our full editorial policy.
